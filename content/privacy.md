## Introduction

Welcome to mtthw.xyz. This personal website is an informational page about my work.

## How the site works

You can read and navigate the site without JavaScript. When JavaScript is available, it can load the site's own JSON pages to update content without a full page reload and animate the background. These features do not track visitors.

## Theme preference

The theme defaults to System, which follows your device's light or dark appearance setting. You can also choose Light or Dark. The site does not save your choice in cookies, local storage, or session storage; it only keeps the selection in the current page while you use it.

## Data collection

I do not ask visitors to provide personal information through this site.

- I do not use cookies.
- I do not use analytics or tracking tools.
- There are no contact forms, user accounts, or newsletter sign-ups.

## Third-party services

This website is hosted by OVH. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, in server logs for security and normal server operation. I do not access or use those logs.

Links to other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

## Changes to this policy

If I add features that collect visitor information, I will update this page accordingly.
