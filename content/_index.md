# 👋 Hi, I’m Matthew.

I’m a software developer working as the Development Support Technical Lead at the [ARC Hub for ICT](https://ict.archub.ie/), hosted by TU Dublin.

The Arc Hub helps researchers move ICT and AI research towards practical products and services. My role is to support research teams with technical planning, software architecture, and development practices as their ideas take shape.

Before joining, I worked at Trinity College Dublin as part of the ADAPT Centre. You can read about that work on the [ADAPT](/adapt/) page and in my [publications](/publications/).
