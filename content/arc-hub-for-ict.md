The [Research Ireland ARC Hub for ICT](https://ict.archub.ie/), hosted by TU Dublin, helps researchers bring ICT and AI research towards practical products and services.

I work as the Hub's Development Support Technical Lead. I support research teams with technical planning, software architecture, and development practices as their ideas take shape.

You can [explore the Hub's projects](https://ict.archub.ie/arc-hub-for-ict-projects/) on its website.
